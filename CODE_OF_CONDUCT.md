# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

FiGuard is a technical project. We expect contributors to:

- Be direct and specific — prefer "this approach has problem X" over vague criticism
- Assume good intent in code reviews and issue discussions
- Keep discussion focused on the technical problem, not the person
- Accept that maintainers make final calls on design decisions

We do not tolerate harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions, or community channels).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates these standards, email **conduct@figuard.io**. Reports are confidential.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the project depending on severity and history.
